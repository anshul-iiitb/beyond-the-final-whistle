**Intro**

You are playing the role of a football player starting his career.
You were just called up to the national team and want to give it your best.

So you look for a club where you can train, learn from more experienced players and steadily improve until you get regular matchtime.

Imagine the layout of this game like a campus with several locations. You can only travel between them using the -travel command inside a location.

Each location has own purpose, activities, commands and cooldowns.

Quick overview of most important locations:

Training Center ( -travel tc )
Global Market ( -travel gm )
Intel Hub ( -travel ih )
Photoshooting Hall ( -travel ph )
Player Sauna ( -travel ps )
Local Club ( -travel lc )
Research Facility ( -travel rf )
Vehicle Showroom ( -travel vs )

The following guides will describe the activities from each location one by one.

**Training & Player Development**

Training is your main objective and the way to improve as a player.

To start go to the Training Center by using the -travel tc command in any location.
Once there use the -train command to open the menu.

Your aim is to get as many Training Points (TP) as possible.
Everybody's TP get reset after every matchday regardless if you play or not.

You can have a maximum of 3 training types:

- Normal Training (gives 1–2 TP/session in the beginning, cooldown 30 minutes)
- VIP training (gives 6 TP/session, cooldown 6 hrs)
- No effort training (can be done x1/day, gives 20 TP for VIP or 50 TP for Premium players)

Some training types are only available to VIP members. 
**Important:** *No Effort* Training blocks both *Normal* and *VIP* Training for the rest of the matchday.

Developing your player

WITS now uses 15 individual attributes to determine how your player performs.

Not every attribute is equally useful for every player. 12 of the 15 can help depending on your position and playstyle. The other 3 do not contribute in the same way.

Some attributes are more important than others, but this doesn't mean the less important ones are useless.

For example, a midfielder can improve his Marking. This might not increase his displayed midfield rating, but it can still help him defend against opposing players and win possession back.

The same applies to other attributes: a skill can affect your performance in a match even if it doesn't increase your OVR.

**Stamina and Recovery**

Your player needs energy to take part in activities. In WITS, that energy is called Stamina.

Your maximum stamina is 100. Activities consume different amounts, so keep an eye on your balance.

You can check your current stamina anytime using the **-profile** command.

**Recovering stamina**

There are three ways to recover stamina:

**1. Daily recovery**

You receive +20 stamina during the daily reset.

**2. Player Sauna**

Travel to the Player Sauna using **-travel ps**, then use:

**-recovery**

- Free recovery.
- Restores +30 stamina for regular members.
- Restores full stamina (up to 100) for Premium members.
- 12-hour cooldown.

**3. Sports Drinks**

Travel to the Global Market using **-travel gm**, then use:

**-buy sd**

- Restores +50 stamina.
- Costs 100k 🪙.
- No cooldown.

**Watch your maximum**

*Remember:* max stamina is 100. If you are at 70 stamina and buy a sports drink, you will not get the full +50 you paid for, you will only recover 30. The remaining 20 points are wasted.

**Before a match**

Try to keep at least 50 stamina before playing. Players below 50 are noticeably more likely to suffer an injury during a match.

Managing stamina is part of managing your career. Spending everything on activities can leave you unprepared when matchday arrives.

**Match Preparation**

Your attributes, playstyle and active bonuses shape your performance in WITS' automated match simulations.

**The teams**

Each team has 11 players: 3 human-controlled players (ATT, MID and DEF) and 8 AI players.

- ATT — Attacker
- MID — Midfielder
- DEF — Defender, also covering the goal

**How your player affects the match**

The match engine uses your attributes to judge how you perform in different situations.

WITS has 15 attributes, and 12 can help depending on your position and playstyle. Attributes outside your usual role can still matter.

For example, Finishing helps with scoring chances, Shot Power with long-range shots, and defensive attributes in defensive situations. These are examples, not a complete list. The engine considers attributes throughout the match, not just one position rating.

**Playstyles matter too**

Your playstyle affects how your player behaves and can influence matchups. Some styles may perform better against particular opponents. You can change playstyle through conversion, but there's a 7-day cooldown. No universal best combination is confirmed; different builds can work in different situations.

**MATCHDAY BONUSES**

**Intel**

- Use " -intel " at the Intel Hub ( -travel ih )
- Free, once per matchday.
- Gives a random +1 to +6 stat boost for the next match.
- Doesn't stack and resets after matchday.

**Boots**

- Buy them at the Global Market for 500k coins.
- Last one day and give +10 to the relevant position stat for your next match.
- Effects don't stack.

**Boot types:**

- "-buy strika" — Attack
- "-buy passa" — Midfield
- "-buy bully" — Defence

Intel and boots can be combined.

**Stamina matters**

Arrive with enough stamina. Players below 50 stamina are more likely to suffer an injury.

**Man of the Match**

The match engine awards Man of the Match (MOTM) to the top-rated player. Match best performer gets +2 in a random important attribute and worst performer gets -1 in random important attribute.

**Research Facility**

The Research Facility is where you can spend your Training Points to permanently develop your player or change his playstyle.

Travel there using **-travel rf**

**Spending Training Points(TP)**

You can use Training Points to improve your player's individual attributes.

Each improvement costs 50 Stamina and between 3 and 15 TP.

The TP cost is dynamically calculated based on the server leaderboard for that specific attribute:

- The player with the lowest value in an attribute pays 3 TP.
- The player with the highest value pays 15 TP.
- Other players pay a cost based on where they stand between those two values.

You can check your own attributes using **-profile**, then opening the **Attributes** tab.

To compare yourself with other players, use **-lb** and select **Attributes Leaderboard**. There you can see who leads each individual attribute.

**Changing your playstyle**

You can also use the Research Facility to change your player's playstyle.

Use **-convert** to initiate a conversion.

- Your attributes remain unchanged.
- Your playstyle changes.
- There is a 7-day cooldown between conversions.

The exact conversion rules and available mechanics are still being explored.

Find out more about Playstyles in this guide: https://discord.com/channels/1522107605244313621/1522307255670931619/1537754353958522990

**Reputation & Followers**

An important part of any world-class player's career is managing his public image.

Whether it's sharing content, connecting with fans or choosing the right sponsors, every decision can affect your number of followers.

**Tweets**

One way to gain followers is to share tweets with the player fanbase in the designated Discord channel.

Each reaction affects your followers:

- 👍 Like: +100k followers
- 👎 Dislike: −50k followers

Tweets don't consume stamina.

Hints:

- Don't take 👎 too hard. You can never please everybody!
- Your main focus should be an increasing trend: more followers at the end of the day than at the start.
- Avoid sending blank tweets. Fans want something interesting to react to.

**Meet your fans**

Another way to connect with your fanbase is to visit your Local Club ( **-travel lc** ) and spend some time with them.

Use **-meetgreet** to sign autographs and meet fans.

- Gives +50k followers.
- Costs 10 stamina.
- Has a 2-hour cooldown.

**Lost Relevancy**

Just like in real life, fans want fresh content.

Players who don't share anything new and interesting become less relevant. In WITS, this is represented by Followers lost to Lost Relevancy during the overnight reset.

The regular loss is 5% of your followers.

So try to keep things engaging for your fans and focus on steady growth rather than worrying about every lost follower.

**Why reputation matters**

Adverts require a minimum number of followers, so building your reputation early opens the door to better sponsorship opportunities.

You can learn more about sponsors, their requirements and rewards in the Money & Sponsorships guide:
